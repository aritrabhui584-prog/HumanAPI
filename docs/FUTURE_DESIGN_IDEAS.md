# HumanAPI Future Design Ideas & Frontend Exploration

## 1. Concept Exploration

This document outlines conceptual UI/UX design evolutions for future major version milestones of HumanAPI.

---

## 2. Strategic Design Concepts

### A. Dynamic Consultation Stage Layouts
- **Pair Programming View**: Split-screen editor with synchronized language syntax highlighting alongside HD video feed.
- **System Architecture Canvas**: Integrated SVG whiteboard for live topology diagramming during 15-minute deep-dive consultations.

### B. Interactive Expert Cards & Video Intro Snippets
- **Hover Video Snippets**: 3-second muted preview clips of experts introducing their domain methodology upon card hover.
- **Live Availability Badges**: Pulsing green status rings indicating experts currently sitting in the instant-connect queue.

### C. Advanced Consultation Post-Game Summaries
- **Key Takeaways Checklist**: Auto-generated action plan checkbox widget saved directly to the user project dashboard.
- **Audio Highlights Snippet**: 15-second snippet bookmarking the exact timestamp where the key technical blocker was resolved.
